It's here: starting today, anyone can get PUBG Stream Report on [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.floerwerth.pubgstreamerencounters). The whole thing started as a script for me and my friends. Then came the closed test, and what the testers said is in the app now.

So: you enter your PUBG name and see if you've ever shown up on a streamer's stream. One tap, and the VOD jumps right to that moment. For the iPhone, the app is still in review at Apple. We'll see how long that takes.
